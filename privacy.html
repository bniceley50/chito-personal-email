Chito Personal Email

Privacy policy

Updated September 21, 2026. This policy describes the Chito Personal Email integration.

Information accessed and used

The integration uses the connected Google account’s verified email identity and OAuth authorization to send messages explicitly requested by its owner. It processes the intended recipient, subject, message text, and Gmail’s send response. It requests Gmail send permission; it does not request inbox-reading permission, download inbox messages, or obtain contacts.

How email is composed and sent

The owner’s request and relevant private conversation context may be processed by models running on the owner’s local infrastructure to compose an email. Google authorization credentials are held by the local controller and are not provided to those models. The controller submits the requested email to Google for delivery to the designated recipient. Requests made through the assistant’s private Telegram chat are also processed by Telegram under its own policies.

Storage and retention

The local controller stores authorization credentials in protected files. Local outbox records retain recipients, subjects, message bodies, attempt status, and any message identifier returned by Google. Outbox records are included in encrypted backups on the owner’s infrastructure; Google authorization credentials are excluded from those backups. Saved outbox records are retained until the owner removes them. Backup copies remain until removed or replaced under the owner’s backup retention process. Sent messages may also remain in Gmail and in recipients’ mailboxes.

Sharing and limited use

Information is used to provide the requested personal email function, maintain its saved outbox, and recover its records. The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Google user data is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Email content is shared with Google and the recipients the owner designates for delivery; private chat requests use Telegram. The integration does not provide other users access to the owner’s Gmail credentials or outbox.

Owner controls and deletion

The owner can revoke Chito’s Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not recall emails already sent or erase locally retained records. The owner can remove local credentials and outbox records through the administrator who manages the Chito installation. Removal from backup copies must be handled separately. Gmail and recipients control their own retained email copies.

Questions

This personal integration is operated by the owner of the website hosting this page. The owner can raise privacy questions through the existing private Chito chat or with the administrator who manages the installation.
