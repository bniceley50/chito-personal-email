Chito Personal Email

Terms of use

Updated September 21, 2026.

Chito Personal Email is a private integration operated for its owner’s personal use. It is not offered as a public email platform and this website does not register users or provide access to the integration.

The owner controls the connected Gmail account and is responsible for the recipient and content of each requested email. A request to send an email invokes sending; a request to draft an email saves an unsent local draft. The integration reports Google’s acceptance when a Gmail message identifier is returned. That response does not guarantee delivery to the recipient’s inbox or that the email was read.

If a send response is lost, delivery may be unconfirmed. The owner should check Gmail’s Sent folder before requesting another copy. The integration does not automatically retry an ambiguous send.

The current version supports one recipient and plain-text content. It does not support attachments, CC/BCC, scheduling, inbox reading, or automatically emailing job-completion results.

Google and Telegram services remain governed by their own terms and policies. See the privacy policy for the information handled by this integration. For questions, the owner can use the existing private Chito chat or contact the administrator who manages the installation.
